Neurodegeneration research campaign

57 runs, 136 hypotheses evaluated, 15 supported

OmiCairn launched 57 research runs on open Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's disease and cross-disease data: single-cell, spatial, genetic and proteomic. Each run that reached planning (33 of 57) pre-registered its hypotheses with decision rules before running analyses, without manual edits between stages. Most hypotheses were not supported or could not be tested. Every outcome is published here, with its evidence, so that others do not repeat the same analyses.

Hypothesis outcomes

Most hypotheses were not supported

The 27 runs that completed at least one test evaluated 136 hypotheses against their pre-registered decision rules. 15 (11%) met the rule. 83 (61%) were not supported or could not be tested with the available data. Even if every partially supported and inconclusive hypothesis counted as support, the total would be at most 53 of 136 (39%).

"Evaluated" includes the 38 hypotheses that could not be tested for lack of suitable data. Another 43 hypotheses belong to runs that never executed them; they are listed in the hypothesis explorer as "not tested (run did not execute)" and are not counted here.

How verdicts were assigned

AI adjudication agents, not human experts, assigned every verdict. For each hypothesis an agent read the run's latest outputs and applied the run's own pre-registered decision rule. Each verdict is backed by a verbatim quote from the run outputs, machine-checked against its source.

The adjudication was deliberately strict. Proxy or surrogate analyses do not count as tests, and where the data did not meet the rule the verdict follows the data, even when the run's own report claimed support. Each run page shows the quote and the adjudicator's note.

Attrition

How far the runs got

Every launch is counted, including dry-run tests and false starts. Of the 57 launches, 17 went through the whole pipeline. No run stopped before planning because data were unavailable; data limits show up later, inside executed runs, as hypotheses that could not be tested.

Reaching review is not a quality verdict

"Completed the pipeline" means a run reached the multi-agent review stage. The reviewer agents are part of OmiCairn. When two external domain reviewers scored seven campaign reports with the same rubric, they scored them consistently lower than the internal reviewer agents did. Expert review is still needed to judge scientific validity.
