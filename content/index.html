A 57-run neurodegeneration research campaign

136 hypotheses evaluated. 15 supported.

OmiCairn is an accountable multi-agent system for hypothesis-driven biomedical omics analysis. We ran it on open Parkinson's and Alzheimer's data and published every outcome, including every null, negative and untestable result.

The campaign

From 57 launches to 15 supported hypotheses

Runs were designed to go through scoping, planning, execution, deterministic cross-checking, claim review and multi-agent review without manual edits between stages. Most stopped early or ended in a null. We count all of them.

Why publish the nulls

Most hypotheses failed. That is the point.

Of 136 evaluated hypotheses, 15 were supported and 7 partially supported. We publish the other 114 with the same care, because a result nobody can see gets tested again.

45

A null is a result

These tests ran against a decision rule fixed in advance, and the rule was not met. Knowing that saves the next group the same analysis.

38

So is a data gap

These hypotheses could not be tested for lack of suitable public data. Together they map where open neurodegeneration data run out.

31

Undecided is not supported

These tests ran but were underpowered, ambiguous or confounded, so the data cannot settle them. They are counted as inconclusive, not as support.

How it works

Every claim is traceable

Decision rules are fixed before the analysis runs. Results then pass three layers of checking: a code-only cross-check, a claim review of every report sentence, and reviewer agents. Expert review is still needed to judge validity.

Plan and runThree layers of checking

  1. 1

    Question

    A research question in plain language, on open data.

  2. 2

    Pre-registered plan

    Hypotheses and their decision rules are fixed before any analysis runs.

  3. 3

    Execution

    Analysis code runs and every intermediate result is recorded.

  4. 4

    Deterministic cross-check

    Code alone, without a language model, checks the results against the pre-registered plan.

  5. 5

    Claim review

    A claim-review agent audits each report sentence against the evidence: 2,766 sentences, 240 issues.

  6. 6

    Reviewer agents

    Multi-agent review sends weak reports back. 19 of 32 executing runs re-ran analyses after checks or review.

Human experts sit outside this pipeline. Campaign outputs were not checked by human experts. In a separate evaluation, two external reviewers scored seven reports lower than the reviewer agents did.
